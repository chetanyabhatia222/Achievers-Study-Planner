About Us

Our Mission

Achievers Academic Planner was built with a simple mission: to help students stay organized, focused, and on top of their academic journey. We believe that effective planning is the foundation of academic success.

Our platform provides an intuitive task management system, subject tracking, and GPA calculation tools — all in one place. Whether you're managing assignments, tracking deadlines, or monitoring your grades, Achievers has you covered.

What We Offer

  • Subject-wise task management with priority levels
  • Real-time progress tracking with visual progress bars
  • Built-in GPA calculator with Indian grading system
  • Dark and light theme for comfortable usage
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
