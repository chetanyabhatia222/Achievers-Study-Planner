Task Management by Subject

GPA Calculator with Grade Breakdown

Enter your marks out of 100 for each subject and get your GPA on a 10-point scale instantly. The breakdown shows the grade letter and points for every subject so you know exactly where you lost marks.

Subjects sync automatically — add a subject in Tasks and it immediately appears in the calculator. No double entry.

03

Weekly Timetable — Click to Fill

A Mon–Fri grid with 7 class periods plus Break and Lunch rows. Click any empty cell, pick a subject from your list, add an optional room number, and it's saved. Hover over a cell to clear it.

The subject dropdown is populated from your saved subjects — so everything stays in sync across all four tools.

Quick Notes — Always Within Reach

Need to write down a formula before class, a reminder about an assignment, or a summary of a lecture? Notes are shown as colour-coded cards, newest first. Click any card to preview the full content.

Type a title, write the body, hit Ctrl+Enter to save. Hover a card to delete. That's the whole workflow.
