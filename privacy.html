Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

Achievers Academic Planner collects minimal information to provide you with the best experience. The information we collect includes:

  • Your name and email address during registration
  • Task and subject data you create within the application
  • Theme preferences and application settings

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve our academic planning services. We use your data to:

  • Display your profile and personalized dashboard
  • Store your tasks, subjects, and academic progress
  • Remember your theme and display preferences

3. Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not transmit your personal data to any external servers. Your information remains on your device and is fully under your control.

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally in your browser, the security of your data depends on your device security. We recommend keeping your device and browser updated to ensure maximum protection.

5. Third-Party Services

Achievers does not share your data with any third-party services, advertisers, or analytics providers. Your academic data is yours alone.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored by the application
  • Modify or update your personal information at any time
  • Delete all your data by clearing your browser's localStorage

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page.
